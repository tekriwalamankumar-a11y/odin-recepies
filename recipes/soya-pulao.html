SOYA PULAO

List of ingredients for 500gm pulao -:

  • Basmati rice (uncooked) - 100 g
  • Dry soya chunks - 60 g
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans) - 100 g
  • Onion - 60 g
  • Tomato - 50 g
  • Low-fat curd - 50 g
  • Cooking oil - 10 g
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 5 g
  • Green chilli - 3 g
  • Cumin seeds - 2 g
  • Garam masala - 2 g
  • Turmeric powder - 1 g
  • Red chilli powder - 1 g
  • Salt - 3 g
  • Water - as required

Follow the follwing steps-:

  1. Wash 100 g basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 15-20 minutes.
    2. "Soaking the rice helps the grains cook more evenly and remain separate."
  2. Boil 60 g dry soya chunks in water for 5-7 minutes.
    3. "Squeeze the cooked soya chunks thoroughly after boiling to remove excess water and improve their texture."
  3. Heat 10 g cooking oil in a pan and add 2 g cumin seeds.
    4. "Allow the cumin seeds to crackle before adding the other ingredients."
  4. Add 60 g chopped onion and sauté until lightly golden.
    5. "Avoid burning the onions because burnt onions can make the pulao bitter."
  5. Add 5 g ginger-garlic paste and 3 g green chilli, then sauté for about 1 minute.
    6. "Cooking the ginger-garlic paste briefly removes its raw smell."
  6. Add 50 g chopped tomato, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
    7. "Cook until the tomato becomes soft and the spices are evenly combined."
  7. Add 100 g mixed vegetables and the boiled soya chunks.
    8. "Stir well so the vegetables and soya absorb the spices evenly."
  8. Add the soaked and drained rice and gently mix everything together.
    9. "Mix gently to avoid breaking the rice grains."
  9. Add approximately 180-200 ml water, cover the pan and cook on low-medium heat until the rice is almost cooked.
    10. "The exact amount of water can vary depending on the rice variety and cooking method."
  10. Add 50 g low-fat curd, gently mix, cover and cook for another 3-5 minutes.
    11. "Adding curd toward the end gives the pulao a slightly creamy texture and mild tang."
  11. Turn off the heat and let the pulao rest, covered, for 5 minutes.
    12. "Resting allows the remaining moisture to distribute evenly through the rice."
  12. Gently fluff the pulao with a fork and serve.
    13. "Fluff from the bottom rather than vigorously stirring to keep the rice grains intact."
