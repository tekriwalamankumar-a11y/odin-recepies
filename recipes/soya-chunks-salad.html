SOYA CHUNKS SALAD

List of ingredients for 500gm salad -:

  • Dry soya chunks - 70 g
  • Cucumber - 120 g
  • Tomato - 100 g
  • Onion - 60 g
  • Carrot - 60 g
  • Capsicum - 40 g
  • Low-fat curd - 30 g
  • Lemon juice - 10 g
  • Green chilli - 3 g
  • Coriander leaves - 5 g
  • Roasted cumin powder - 2 g
  • Black pepper powder - 1 g
  • Salt - 3 g

Follow the follwing steps-:

  1. Boil 70 g dry soya chunks in water for 5-7 minutes until they become soft.
    2. "Boiling hydrates the soya chunks and makes them easier to digest."
  2. Drain the soya chunks and rinse them once with clean water.
    3. "Rinsing helps remove the strong smell and improves the taste."
  3. Squeeze the soya chunks firmly to remove excess water.
    4. "Removing excess water prevents the salad from becoming watery."
  4. Chop the cucumber, tomato, onion, carrot and capsicum into small pieces.
    5. "Cut the vegetables into similarly sized pieces for a better texture in every bite."
  5. Add the squeezed soya chunks and chopped vegetables to a large mixing bowl.
    6. "Use a large bowl so the ingredients can be mixed without crushing the vegetables."
  6. Add 30 g low-fat curd, 10 g lemon juice, green chilli and coriander leaves.
    7. "The curd adds creaminess while lemon juice provides freshness and acidity."
  7. Add roasted cumin powder, black pepper powder and salt.
    8. "Add salt close to serving time to prevent the vegetables from releasing too much water."
  8. Mix everything gently until the dressing coats the soya chunks and vegetables evenly.
    9. "Avoid overmixing because it can make the vegetables soft."
  9. Refrigerate the salad for 10-15 minutes before serving.
    10. "A short chilling period allows the flavours to blend together."
  10. Serve fresh and consume preferably on the same day.
    11. "For the best texture, keep the salad refrigerated until you are ready to eat it."
