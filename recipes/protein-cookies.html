PROTEIN COOKIES

List of ingredients for 250g cookies -:

  • Oats - 80 g
  • Whey protein powder - 50 g
  • Peanut butter - 40 g
  • Banana - 40 g
  • Milk - 20 g
  • Honey - 10 g
  • Dark chocolate chips - 5 g
  • Almonds - 3 g
  • Baking powder - 1 g
  • Cinnamon powder - 1 g

Follow the follwing steps-:

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
    2. "Mash the banana thoroughly until there are no large lumps."
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine oats, whey protein powder, baking powder, and cinnamon powder.
    3. "Mix the dry ingredients evenly so the protein powder does not form pockets."
  3. Add mashed banana, peanut butter, milk, and honey to the dry mixture.
    4. "Stir gently until you get a thick, slightly sticky cookie dough."
  4. Fold in the chopped almonds and dark chocolate chips.
    5. "If the dough feels too dry, add 5-10 g of milk gradually."
  5. Divide the dough into equal portions and shape them into cookies on the baking tray.
    6. "Flatten each cookie slightly because these cookies will not spread much during baking."
  6. Bake at 175°C for approximately 12-15 minutes, or until the edges become lightly golden.
    7. "Do not overbake, as whey protein can make cookies dry and hard."
  7. Remove the cookies from the oven and allow them to cool on the tray for 5-10 minutes.
    8. "Letting them cool helps the cookies firm up and develop a better texture."
  8. Transfer the cookies to a wire rack and allow them to cool completely before storing.
