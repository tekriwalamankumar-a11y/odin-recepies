PROTEIN SHAKE

List of ingredients for 300ml shake -:

  • Skimmed milk - 180 g
  • Whey protein powder - 30 g
  • Banana - 50 g
  • Roasted oats - 15 g
  • Peanut butter - 8 g
  • Almonds - 3 g
  • Cinnamon powder - 0.5 g
  • Ice cubes - 30 g

Follow the follwing steps-:

  1. Add 15 g roasted oats to a blender and pulse for 10-15 seconds.
    2. "Grinding the oats first prevents a grainy texture."
  2. Add 180 g chilled skimmed milk.
    3. "Keep the milk cold so you need less ice and retain a thicker texture."
  3. Add 50 g banana and 8 g peanut butter.
    4. "Use a ripe banana for natural sweetness instead of adding sugar."
  4. Add 30 g whey protein powder and 0.5 g cinnamon powder.
    5. "Add whey after the liquid ingredients to reduce powder sticking to the blender."
  5. Add 3 g almonds and 30 g ice cubes.
    6. "You can skip the almonds if you want a smoother shake and slightly fewer calories."
  6. Blend for 30-45 seconds until completely smooth.
    7. "If the shake becomes too thick, add 10-20 ml cold water and blend again."
  7. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.
    8. "For the best taste and texture, consume the shake soon after preparation."
